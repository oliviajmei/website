my dream

the idea

I’m motivated by making learning more accessible on an individual level. My long-term dream is to build schools that use robotics and technology to support teachers in overcrowded classrooms, identifying when students are struggling and helping provide personalized support. I believe people are naturally capable of learning, but traditional education can make students feel “behind” when they do not fit a predetermined pace. I want to create environments where students feel capable and excited to learn. I’ve already started pursuing this through an early-stage educational software I built that uses learning science, spaced reinforcement, diagnostic questions, and adaptive learning paths to personalize education.
